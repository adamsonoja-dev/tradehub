Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

Candleflow
What is Demo mode?

Demo mode lets you practice with simulated funds without financial risk.

Can I trade live?

Live trading is disabled for now. Candleflow currently offers demo trading only, along with market conversion and profit-and-loss estimates.

Who can access Admin?

Only authenticated users with the server-side Admin role can access administrative data.

How do stop losses and take profits work?

Set a stop loss to automatically close a losing trade at a price you choose, and a take profit to lock in gains at your target. Both are available on the order ticket.

How does the account balance work?

Your demo balance starts at $0. Use the Account Funding panel on the dashboard to deposit (or withdraw) simulated funds instantly.
