Order types

How the ticket works

Market order

Executes immediately at the current market price. Ideal when you want to be in the trade right now.

Limit order

Executes only if the market reaches your chosen entry price. Useful for planning entries ahead of time.

Stop loss

A price that automatically closes your trade to limit losses. The best habit to build early.

Take profit

A price that automatically locks in gains by closing a winning trade at your target.
